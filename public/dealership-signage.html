Dealership campaign concept

Built around experience with a local dealership.

Erik’s signage background includes sales, project management, banners, graphics and sign installation for a local automotive dealership. SolidSite Signs brings that experience to dealerships and businesses in Greater Houston that need a practical contact for repeat signage work.

You work directly with Erik on the project brief. Production is coordinated with outside suppliers, then suitable installation or local delivery is agreed around the job. The point is to keep the message, artwork and on-site details in the same conversation.

Plan the promotion as a set.

Campaign banners
Identify the approved message, dimensions and display locations. Custom banners can be scoped alongside the other pieces needed for the campaign.
Showroom and window graphics
Review each panel, entrance and existing graphic before preparing replacements. Window graphics should be coordinated with the same campaign artwork.
Installation and delivery
Include the preferred installation window, site contact and access needs. We confirm the installation or delivery scope before scheduling.

A useful brief for a repeat campaign.

Send a location-by-location list of the signs being changed, the new creative and the campaign dates. Note which pieces can keep the same dimensions and which need new measurements. If several people approve the artwork, identify the person who gives final sign-off.

For replacement work, include photos of what is currently installed and explain whether removal is needed. Confirm access around showroom activity, customer entrances and receiving hours. A clear changeover plan makes it easier to quote the actual work.

Each order still receives its own confirmed scope and timing. We do not assume earlier prices, supplier availability or artwork approvals carry over to a new promotion. Use the sign project checklist to organize the information before sending the next request.

Before you order

Can we use one contact for banners and graphics?+

Yes. Include the full signage list in one request so artwork, production, delivery and suitable installation can be reviewed together.

Can you help with recurring promotions?+

Yes. We can discuss the upcoming campaign schedule and repeat signage needs. Scope, artwork approval, production availability and timing are confirmed for each order.

Do you need the manufacturer-approved artwork?+

Send any required brand guidelines and approved creative you already have. Tell us who approves the final proof. Brand and offer approval remains with your dealership before production.
