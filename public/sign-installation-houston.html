Mounted sign concept

The surface is part of the project.

Installation starts with the sign, the place it will go and how that area can be reached. A wall-mounted panel, a banner and an adhesive graphic require different preparation. Photos and dimensions help us determine a suitable approach and what information is still needed.

SolidSite’s experience includes banners, graphics and sign installs for a local dealership. Erik handles project coordination and suitable installation or delivery. Work requiring specialist equipment, trades or additional approvals is reviewed before any commitment.

Tell us which part you need handled.

Sign and banner installation
Share the sign dimensions, material, approximate weight and mounting details. Include a wide photo of the location and closer photos of the proposed attachment area.
Graphic installation
Show us the surface, panel dimensions and any existing graphics. Note whether the material is already produced or needs to be coordinated as part of the project.
Local sign delivery
If you have installation covered, ask about delivery. Provide the destination, receiving contact, sign dimensions and an appropriate receiving window.

What we review before scheduling.

The scope depends on surface condition, mounting height, access, existing hardware, removal needs and the installation window. Include any property requirements and tell us who can approve access at the site. We confirm which work is included before a date is agreed.

If your sign is being made elsewhere, send its specifications and any supplier installation guidance. Compatibility with the location needs review; an install-only request is not automatically accepted because the sign is already finished.

Need the sign produced too? Explore custom banners or window graphics. We can discuss production coordination and installation together, with one point of contact for the approved scope.

Match the sign to the mounting surface

A corrugated yard sign needs a different support from an acrylic wall panel. If you already have hardware, compare sign standoffs or yard-sign stakes and supports before sending photos of the proposed installation.

Before you order

Can you install a sign I already have?+

Send photos, dimensions, material and any manufacturer instructions. We’ll review whether the sign and location are suitable and what the installation requires before accepting the job.

Can you quote installation from photos?+

Photos are useful for the initial review. Some locations need measurements, additional details or a site review before the scope and price can be confirmed.

Do you offer delivery without installation?+

Yes, local delivery can be discussed separately. The destination, sign size, handling needs and receiving arrangements determine the agreed delivery scope.

Bring us
the details.

A rough idea is enough to get started. These details help us prepare a useful quote.

  • Sign type, dimensions and approximate weight
  • Photos of the sign and mounting surface
  • Mounting height and how the area is accessed
  • Existing hardware or graphics to remove
  • Site address, receiving contact and target date
