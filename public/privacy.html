Your information.

This page describes the information collected through the SolidSite Signs website. For questions, contact erik@gosolidsite.com or (713) 912-2151.

Project inquiries

The quote form asks for your name, email, project location and project details. Business name, phone number, delivery preference and timing help us understand the request. Your submission is stored as a project inquiry so SolidSite can review it and respond.

How project information is used

Information you submit is used to discuss your project, prepare a quote and coordinate requested services. Relevant project specifications may need to be shared with production or installation providers when preparing or fulfilling your project. Please do not include payment card details, passwords or other sensitive personal information in the form.

Website operation

If you arrive from a tagged link, we keep its campaign labels, landing page and any Google ad click identifier in this site's tab session. If you submit a quote, we save that information with the inquiry to understand which ads lead to projects. We do not send your form details to advertising platforms or run third-party analytics scripts on this site. The server temporarily uses connection information to limit abusive submissions. The hosting provider may maintain ordinary technical logs. Fonts and site imagery are served with the website.

Email and phone

Email and telephone links open your own email or calling application. Information sent that way is handled by the relevant communications providers and SolidSite’s business accounts. The website does not send automatic marketing messages when you request a quote.

Questions and requests

Contact erik@gosolidsite.com to ask about your submitted information or request a correction or deletion. Include your project reference if you have one.
