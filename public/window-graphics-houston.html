Window graphic concept

Make the entrance work harder.

A window graphic can introduce the business, explain an offer or help someone find the right entrance. The useful starting point is the message—not filling every inch of glass. Share what customers need to see and which areas need to remain clear.

SolidSite handles the project details and coordinates outside production. Our experience includes graphics and installation for a local automotive dealership. That experience informs a practical approach to updating windows while keeping the location and its next promotion in mind.

Different graphics for different jobs.

Business lettering
Your business name, entrance information or operating hours. Confirm the exact text before artwork is prepared, especially phone numbers and opening times.
Promotional window graphics
A campaign message sized to the glass and its viewing distance. Tell us when the promotion starts, when it ends and whether another message will replace it.
Showroom graphics
Coordinate the windows with other promotional signage. For recurring automotive work, see dealership banners and graphics.

A photo helps. Measurements make it specific.

Send a straight-on photo of the full window or door and a closer photo of the surface. Include the width and height of each glass panel, frames or dividers, door handles and any existing graphics. Mark the areas you want to use.

Let us know whether the graphic should be read from outside, how long it will stay in place and whether old material needs to be removed. Existing film, coatings, surface condition and access can affect the material and installation approach; these need to be reviewed before anything is produced.

Artwork preparation, panel sizes, quantity, removal and installation requirements all affect pricing. If the project also needs a promotional banner, include it in the same brief so the messages can be coordinated.

Before you order

Can you make business hours or door lettering?+

Yes. Send the exact text, logo if needed, a photo of the door and the available glass dimensions. The material and layout are confirmed during the quote process.

Can you replace existing window graphics?+

We can review the replacement. Include clear photos of the old graphics and glass, and tell us whether removal is part of the request. Removal and surface condition need to be assessed separately.

Do I need finished artwork?+

No. A logo and a clear description are enough to start a conversation. We’ll identify the artwork preparation needed and include that work in the scope before proceeding.
